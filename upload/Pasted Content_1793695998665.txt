# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT: COMPLETE ONLINE TRAINING & COURSE MANAGEMENT PLATFORM

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Develop and refine a complete, professional Online Training and Course Management Platform.

The platform will allow an administrator to create and sell training courses, organize lessons into topics, upload recorded videos and learning materials, manage student subscriptions, assign course-specific assignments, receive student submissions and provide certificates of completion.

Students will have their own accounts and personalized dashboards where they can access eligible courses, watch lessons, download materials, complete assignments and access their certificates.

The platform must have three main experiences:

1. Public Website – For visitors to explore the platform, courses and subscription plans and register.
2. Student Portal – For students to access courses, watch lessons, manage subscriptions, submit assignments and view certificates.
3. Admin Dashboard – For administrators to manage the entire platform, students, courses, videos, topics, subscriptions, assignments and certificates.

### Critical development instructions

- Work on the existing project.
- First inspect the complete existing codebase and understand its architecture.
- Preserve all existing working functionality.
- Do not rebuild the application from scratch.
- Do not introduce duplicate authentication systems.
- Reuse the existing database, backend and authentication wherever possible.
- Implement real functionality rather than static UI demonstrations.
- Connect every form, button, table and action to the actual backend.
- Do not use fake statistics or hardcoded demonstration records.
- Make the entire platform responsive.
- Test all workflows and permissions before considering the implementation complete.

## 21. Full Video Watching Requirement

THIS IS ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS.

A student must watch the entire video before the lesson can be marked as completed.

Simply opening the video must NOT mark it as completed.

Implement actual video completion tracking.

### Required Working Logic

1. Student opens a lesson.
2. The video starts playing.
3. The system tracks playback progress.
4. Progress is periodically saved to the backend.
5. The system tracks the actual watched duration.
6. When the video reaches its end and the required viewing conditions are satisfied, mark the lesson as completed.
7. Display a completion tick beside the lesson.
8. Save the completion record in the database.

### Important Restrictions

- Do not mark a lesson complete just because the student opens it.
- Do not mark a lesson complete when the video is paused halfway.
- Do not mark it complete merely because the student seeks directly to the end.
- Do not mark it complete if the student closes the page before finishing.
- Do not allow frontend-only manipulation to grant completion.

Use the video player's playback events and backend validation.

For supported video players, track:
- Current playback time.
- Total video duration.
- Actual playback progress.
- Video ended event.
- Watched time.
- Completion status.

Use periodic progress saving.

When a student returns to a partially watched video, restore the last saved position.

For externally hosted videos, implement the strongest tracking supported by the selected player/API. Clearly account for limitations where the external provider does not expose reliable playback events.

### Completed Lesson Display

When a video is completed:
- Display a green checkmark.
- Change lesson status to Completed.
- Save the completion timestamp.
- Preserve completion if the student logs out or returns later.

Incomplete lessons should retain their saved progress.

- Topic 2: 4 videos.

- Topic 4: 3 videos.

The assignment must remain locked until all 12 required videos have been completed.

- Display downloadable assignment file.

- Display Upload Completed Assignment button.

## 30. Certificate Unlocking and Issuing Logic

THIS IS AN IMPORTANT WORKFLOW.

The certificate must be associated with a particular course.

The required workflow is:

1. Student completes every required video lesson.
2. All topics become completed.
3. Course assignment becomes available.
4. Student downloads the assignment.
5. Student completes and uploads the assignment.
6. The system records the assignment submission.
7. The certificate becomes available to the student according to the configured certificate settings.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE:

For this version, the certificate should become available immediately after successful assignment upload.

The admin does NOT need to approve or review the assignment before the certificate becomes available.

Assignment review is a separate administrative feature and must not block certificate issuance.

If certificates are disabled for a course, do not issue one.

# PART 16: SECURITY AND ACCESS CONTROL

## 45. Role-Based Access

The platform must distinguish between administrators and students.

### Admin
Can manage:
- Students.
- Courses.
- Topics.
- Videos.
- Learning materials.
- Subscription plans.
- Assignments.
- Submissions.
- Certificates.

### Student
Can:
- Access eligible courses.
- Watch lessons.
- View learning materials.
- Track their own progress.
- Download unlocked assignments.
- Upload their own submissions.
- View their own certificates.
- View their own subscription.

Students must not be able to:

- Access admin routes.
- Modify their subscription status.
- Modify their expiry date.
- Mark lessons as completed manually.
- Access another student's files.
- Download locked assignments.
- Generate unauthorized certificates.

## 46. Backend Enforcement

All important access restrictions must be enforced on the backend.

This includes:

- Subscription validity.
- Free/paid course permissions.
- Eligible subscription plans.
- Lesson completion.
- Assignment unlocking.
- Assignment submissions.
- Certificate issuing.
- Student-specific data access.

# PART 17: EXACT END-TO-END WORKFLOWS

## 47. Complete Admin Workflow

1. Admin logs into the admin dashboard.
2. Creates subscription plans.
3. Sets prices, durations and features.
4. Activates plans.
5. Creates a course.
6. Selects Free or Paid.
7. If Paid, assigns eligible plans.
8. Adds topics to the course.
9. Uploads or links video lessons.
10. Assigns each video to its topic.
11. Adds PDFs and other learning materials.
12. Creates an assignment for the course.
13. Uploads the assignment document.
14. Configures a certificate for the course.
15. Publishes the course.

## 48. Complete Student Workflow

1. Student visits the public website.
2. Explores courses and learning plans.
3. Registers an account.
4. Selects a subscription plan.
5. Receives an active subscription assignment without online payment processing.
6. Enters the student dashboard.
7. Views available free and eligible paid courses.
8. Opens a course.
9. Views its topics and video lessons.
10. Watches each video completely.
11. The system saves lesson completion.
12. Completed videos display checkmarks.
13. Once all videos in a topic are completed, that topic is marked completed.
14. Once all required course lessons are completed, the course assignment unlocks.
15. Student downloads the assignment.
16. Completes the assignment.
17. Uploads the completed file.
18. The submission is recorded and visible in the admin dashboard.
19. The certificate becomes available immediately after successful assignment submission, if certificates are enabled for the course.
20. Student views and downloads the certificate.

## 49. Complete Subscription Expiry Workflow

1. Student's subscription reaches its expiry timestamp.
2. Backend identifies the expired subscription.
3. Status changes to Expired.
4. Protected dashboard access is restricted.
5. Paid course access is blocked.
6. Protected learning materials become inaccessible.
7. Student sees the expiry screen.
8. Student can select a new available plan.
9. Once a new plan is assigned, access is restored according to the new plan.

Previous learning history, assignment submissions and issued certificates must remain preserved.

# PART 19: FINAL TESTING REQUIREMENTS

## 53. Functional Testing

Test all the following scenarios:

### Subscription Tests
- Student selects a one-month plan.
- Student selects a longer-duration plan.
- Subscription expiry is calculated correctly.
- Expired student loses protected access.
- New subscription restores eligible access.
- Admin changes a plan.
- Deactivated plan is no longer available to new registrations.
- Existing student records remain preserved.

### Course Tests
- Free course is accessible to registered students.
- Paid course is accessible to eligible active subscribers.
- Paid course is inaccessible to ineligible plans.
- Expired subscribers cannot access paid courses.
- Unpublished courses are not publicly available.

### Video Tests
- Opening a video does not mark it complete.
- Watching only part of a video does not mark it complete.
- Seeking to the end does not automatically mark it complete.
- Completing the required video playback marks it complete.
- Progress is saved.
- Returning to a lesson restores progress.
- Completed videos retain their checkmarks.

### Assignment Tests
- Assignment remains locked while required lessons are incomplete.
- Assignment unlocks after all required lessons are completed.
- Unauthorized students cannot download it.
- Student can upload a supported file.
- Submission is associated with the correct student.
- Admin can view and download the submission.

### Certificate Tests
- Certificate cannot be issued before assignment submission.
- Certificate becomes available immediately after successful submission.
- Admin review does not block certificate availability.
- Disabled certificate settings prevent issuance.
- Certificates are linked to the correct student and course.
- Duplicate certificates are not generated.

### Security Tests
- Student cannot access admin pages.
- Student cannot change subscription expiry.
- Student cannot modify another student's progress.
- Student cannot download another student's assignment submission.
- Student cannot access unauthorized courses or certificates.
