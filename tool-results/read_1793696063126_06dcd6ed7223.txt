     1→     1→# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT: COMPLETE ONLINE TRAINING & COURSE MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
     2→     2→
     3→     3→## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
     4→     4→
     5→     5→Develop and refine a complete, professional Online Training and Course Management Platform.
     6→     6→
     7→     7→The platform will allow an administrator to create and sell training courses, organize lessons into topics, upload recorded videos and learning materials, manage student subscriptions, assign course-specific assignments, receive student submissions and provide certificates of completion.
     8→     8→
     9→     9→Students will have their own accounts and personalized dashboards where they can access eligible courses, watch lessons, download materials, complete assignments and access their certificates.
    10→    10→
    11→    11→The platform must have three main experiences:
    12→    12→
    13→    13→1. Public Website – For visitors to explore the platform, courses and subscription plans and register.
    14→    14→2. Student Portal – For students to access courses, watch lessons, manage subscriptions, submit assignments and view certificates.
    15→    15→3. Admin Dashboard – For administrators to manage the entire platform, students, courses, videos, topics, subscriptions, assignments and certificates.
    16→    16→
    17→    17→### Critical development instructions
    18→    18→
    19→    19→- Work on the existing project.
    20→    20→- First inspect the complete existing codebase and understand its architecture.
    21→    21→- Preserve all existing working functionality.
    22→    22→- Do not rebuild the application from scratch.
    23→    23→- Do not introduce duplicate authentication systems.
    24→    24→- Reuse the existing database, backend and authentication wherever possible.
    25→    25→- Implement real functionality rather than static UI demonstrations.
    26→    26→- Connect every form, button, table and action to the actual backend.
    27→    27→- Do not use fake statistics or hardcoded demonstration records.
    28→    28→- Make the entire platform responsive.
    29→    29→- Test all workflows and permissions before considering the implementation complete.

   630→   630→Simply opening the video must NOT mark it as completed.
   631→   631→
   632→   632→Implement actual video completion tracking.
   633→   633→
   634→   634→### Required Working Logic
   635→   635→
   636→   636→1. Student opens a lesson.
   637→   637→2. The video starts playing.
   638→   638→3. The system tracks playback progress.
   639→   639→4. Progress is periodically saved to the backend.
   640→   640→5. The system tracks the actual watched duration.
   641→   641→6. When the video reaches its end and the required viewing conditions are satisfied, mark the lesson as completed.
   642→   642→7. Display a completion tick beside the lesson.
   643→   643→8. Save the completion record in the database.
   644→   644→
   645→   645→### Important Restrictions
   646→   646→
   647→   647→- Do not mark a lesson complete just because the student opens it.
   648→   648→- Do not mark a lesson complete when the video is paused halfway.
   649→   649→- Do not mark it complete merely because the student seeks directly to the end.
   650→   650→- Do not mark it complete if the student closes the page before finishing.
   651→   651→- Do not allow frontend-only manipulation to grant completion.
   652→   652→
   653→   653→Use the video player's playback events and backend validation.
   654→   654→
   655→   655→For supported video players, track:
   656→   656→- Current playback time.
   657→   657→- Total video duration.
   658→   658→- Actual playback progress.
   659→   659→- Video ended event.
   660→   660→- Watched time.
   661→   661→- Completion status.
   662→   662→
   663→   663→Use periodic progress saving.
   664→   664→
   665→   665→When a student returns to a partially watched video, restore the last saved position.
   666→   666→
   667→   667→For externally hosted videos, implement the strongest tracking supported by the selected player/API. Clearly account for limitations where the external provider does not expose reliable playback events.
   668→   668→
   669→   669→### Completed Lesson Display
   670→   670→
   671→   671→When a video is completed:
   672→   672→- Display a green checkmark.
   673→   673→- Change lesson status to Completed.
   674→   674→- Save the completion timestamp.
   675→   675→- Preserve completion if the student logs out or returns later.
   676→   676→
   677→   677→Incomplete lessons should retain their saved progress.

  1356→  1356→# PART 17: EXACT END-TO-END WORKFLOWS
  1357→  1357→
  1358→  1358→## 47. Complete Admin Workflow
  1359→  1359→
  1360→  1360→1. Admin logs into the admin dashboard.
  1361→  1361→2. Creates subscription plans.
  1362→  1362→3. Sets prices, durations and features.
  1363→  1363→4. Activates plans.
  1364→  1364→5. Creates a course.
  1365→  1365→6. Selects Free or Paid.
  1366→  1366→7. If Paid, assigns eligible plans.
  1367→  1367→8. Adds topics to the course.
  1368→  1368→9. Uploads or links video lessons.
  1369→  1369→10. Assigns each video to its topic.
  1370→  1370→11. Adds PDFs and other learning materials.
  1371→  1371→12. Creates an assignment for the course.
  1372→  1372→13. Uploads the assignment document.
  1373→  1373→14. Configures a certificate for the course.
  1374→  1374→15. Publishes the course.
  1375→  1375→
  1376→  1376→## 48. Complete Student Workflow
  1377→  1377→
  1378→  1378→1. Student visits the public website.
  1379→  1379→2. Explores courses and learning plans.
  1380→  1380→3. Registers an account.
  1381→  1381→4. Selects a subscription plan.
  1382→  1382→5. Receives an active subscription assignment without online payment processing.
  1383→  1383→6. Enters the student dashboard.
  1384→  1384→7. Views available free and eligible paid courses.
  1385→  1385→8. Opens a course.
  1386→  1386→9. Views its topics and video lessons.
  1387→  1387→10. Watches each video completely.
  1388→  1388→11. The system saves lesson completion.
  1389→  1389→12. Completed videos display checkmarks.
  1390→  1390→13. Once all videos in a topic are completed, that topic is marked completed.
  1391→  1391→14. Once all required course lessons are completed, the course assignment unlocks.
  1392→  1392→15. Student downloads the assignment.
  1393→  1393→16. Completes the assignment.
  1394→  1394→17. Uploads the completed file.
  1395→  1395→18. The submission is recorded and visible in the admin dashboard.
  1396→  1396→19. The certificate becomes available immediately after successful assignment submission, if certificates are enabled for the course.
  1397→  1397→20. Student views and downloads the certificate.
  1398→  1398→
  1399→  1399→## 49. Complete Subscription Expiry Workflow
  1400→  1400→
  1401→  1401→1. Student's subscription reaches its expiry timestamp.
  1402→  1402→2. Backend identifies the expired subscription.
  1403→  1403→3. Status changes to Expired.
  1404→  1404→4. Protected dashboard access is restricted.
  1405→  1405→5. Paid course access is blocked.
  1406→  1406→6. Protected learning materials become inaccessible.
  1407→  1407→7. Student sees the expiry screen.
  1408→  1408→8. Student can select a new available plan.
  1409→  1409→9. Once a new plan is assigned, access is restored according to the new plan.
  1410→  1410→
  1411→  1411→Previous learning history, assignment submissions and issued certificates must remain preserved.
